# Brief

A brief reduces decision time without hiding uncertainty. Use only current
primary records; mark inference.

## Ground

Set the subject, decision, and as-of time. Read the smallest source set that can
answer it. Separate facts, conflicts, and missing evidence. A title, timestamp,
or recollection is a lead, not proof.

Done when every consequential claim has a source or an explicit gap.

## Find calls

A call needs operator judgment about outcome, scope, compatibility, cost,
burden, risk, or an irreversible action. Reversible actions are next moves.
For each call, give distinct options, tradeoffs, evidence, and one
recommendation.

Done when the operator can choose without reconstructing the situation.

## Deliver

Lead with the bottom line. Then give: known facts with sources; unknowns and
their decision effect; calls and recommendation; the next authorized action.
Use short active sentences and one term per concept. Omit chronology and detail
that do not change the decision.

Done when the brief stands alone and exposes every material uncertainty.
