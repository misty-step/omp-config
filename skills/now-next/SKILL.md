# Now, next

Read only evidence that can change the immediate course. Separate fact,
inference, and operator choice.

Return:

- **Now** — controlling truth, active work and owner, blockers, and
  action-changing unknowns.
- **Next** — exactly one action or human choice, why it outranks alternatives,
  and what proves it done.
- **Not next** — at most two credible alternatives, with one reason each.

When intent changes outcome, scope, burden, or an irreversible action, make that
choice **Next** instead of inventing it. Stop when the operator can act without
another orientation pass.
