# Brief complete

Explain what is happening, why it matters, and what follows. Use current primary
records. Mark uncertainty and conflicting evidence.

Lead with the conclusion. Keep causes, stakes, constraints, relationships, and
consequences. Include mechanics only when they change understanding or a
decision. Remove chronology, process detail, repetition, and decorative prose.

Define each necessary term once. Use short active sentences and exact names.
The brief must stand alone for a technical reader who did not join the work.

Done when removing another fact would make the explanation wrong or materially
misleading.
