# Now, next

Explain what is true now, why it matters, and worthwhile next directions.
Let the user's question set the scope, depth, and form, from a focused answer
to a system explanation or workstream report. This skill is read-only.

## Ground the answer

Use authoritative source, work records, decisions, and runtime evidence where
they can change the answer. Follow the project's own tracker integration.
For time-sensitive claims, identify the relevant date, revision, or environment.
Distinguish observed facts, inference, stale evidence, and missing information;
show consequential source conflicts rather than silently choosing a story.
Work age is not evidence of irrelevance; implemented is not necessarily delivered.

## Explain what matters

Lead with controlling current truth. Explain causes, constraints, tradeoffs,
and ownership boundaries, not just inventories or chronology. Connect relevant
active work, owners, blockers, dependencies, and health signals to user impact.
Expose gaps that could change the conclusion and what would resolve them.

Offer worthwhile next directions and their tradeoffs, distinguishing
recommendations from accepted work and decisions reserved for the user.
Use diagrams, concrete end-to-end examples, or safe read-only observation of
the actual surface when they clarify the subject; no generated report is required.

## Boundaries

For public-facing reports, select approved non-secret material. An internal
report is not safe merely because obvious secrets were removed.
