# Now, next

Work drifts when current truth, the next decision, and later possibilities blur.
This read-only pause restores focus without reconstructing the whole project.

Read only evidence that can change the immediate course. Separate fact,
inference, and operator choice.

Return:

- **Now** — the controlling truth, active work and owner, and any blocker or
  action-changing unknown.
- **Next** — exactly one action or human choice, why it outranks alternatives,
  and what proves it done.
- **Not next** — at most two credible alternatives, with one reason each.

When human intent changes the outcome, scope, burden, or an irreversible action,
make that decision **Next** instead of inventing intent.

Done when the operator can act without another orientation pass.
