# /sprites eval waiver

expires: 2026-09-29

## Reason

The acceptance surface needs live Fly Sprites infrastructure and a dedicated
paid sandbox. That external dependency does not yet provide a repeatable
public fixture.

## Disposition

This waiver defers coverage for a limited time; it is not an exemption. Before
expiry, add an eval that provisions a disposable Sprite, proves the
credential-free handoff contract, destroys the sandbox, and records
provider-neutral evidence. Otherwise renew the waiver with a new reason and
date.
