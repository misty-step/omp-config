# Frontier capability receipts

Use these receipts when difficulty causes an agent to reduce the requested outcome.
They refute stale assumptions about model ceilings.
They do not replace evidence, safety controls, approval boundaries, or verification.

All sources were retrieved on 2026-07-30.
The labels distinguish verified papers from first-party organization claims.

## Mathematics

### An internal model disproved the Erdős unit-distance conjecture

**Observed fact.** A paper by nine external mathematicians states that an internal
OpenAI model produced the original proof in one mathematical generation.
The authors then verified, simplified, and generalized it.

> “We present a short, digested, human-verified version of the recent
> OpenAI-generated counterexample to the Erdős unit distance conjecture.”
>
> “The following result is due to an internal model at OpenAI.”

The paper says the proof “was first mathematically generated in one shot” before
humans refined its exposition through Codex interactions.
The result ended a conjecture that followed Erdős's 1946 construction.

**Boundary.** Humans verified and rewrote the result.
The paper does not claim that every generated proof is reliable.

Sources:

- Noga Alon and eight coauthors,
  [“Remarks on the disproof of the unit distance conjecture”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20695),
  2026-05-20.
- OpenAI,
  [“An OpenAI model has disproved a central conjecture in discrete geometry”](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/),
  2026-05-20.

### Fable received credit for work leading to a Jacobian counterexample

**Observed fact.** A verification preprint proves that an explicit polynomial map
is a counterexample in dimension three and all dimensions above three.
The preprint credits Fable for work that led to the example.

> “We verify an explicit polynomial map F : C3 → C3 with constant Jacobian
> determinant −2 and a fiber containing three points, thereby disproving the
> Jacobian conjecture in dimension three and, by stabilization, in every
> dimension at least three.”
>
> “The explicit formula studied below was announced by Levent Alpöge; the cited
> post credits Akhil Mathew for asking the question and Fable for the work
> leading to the example.”

**Boundary.** The preprint verifies the formula but has not completed journal
peer review.
Dimension two remains open.

Source:

- Ulam AI,
  [“The Jacobian Conjecture is False in Dimension Three”](https://www.ulam.ai/research/jacobian.pdf),
  2026-07-20.

### Aletheia produced autonomous open-problem results

**Source claim.** Google DeepMind reports that Aletheia produced one research
paper without human intervention.
It also reports autonomous solutions to four open questions in a 700-problem
Erdős evaluation.

> “A research paper generated by AI without any human intervention, calculating
> certain structure constants in arithmetic geometry called eigenweights.”
>
> “An extensive semi-autonomous evaluation of 700 open problems ... including
> autonomous solutions to four open questions listed there.”

**Boundary.** DeepMind assigns different autonomy and novelty levels to these
results.
Its published table leaves the two highest novelty levels empty.
Human experts graded evaluation results.

Sources:

- Google DeepMind,
  [“Accelerating mathematical and scientific discovery with Gemini Deep Think”](https://deepmind.google/blog/accelerating-mathematical-and-scientific-discovery-with-gemini-deep-think/),
  2026-02-11.
- Google DeepMind researchers,
  [“Towards Autonomous Mathematics Research”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10177),
  2026.

## Cryptography and cybersecurity

### Mythos found a publishable reduced-round AES improvement

**Source claim.** Anthropic reports that Mythos almost autonomously found the
Möbius Bridge improvement against seven-round AES-128.
The work used a long run, a scaffold, many parallel sessions, and repeated
operator corrections that preserved the hard target.

> “why not do aes-128 r7? the whole point is to find something better than
> existing approaches.”
>
> “no again the goal is that we have highly inteligent [sic] model as good top
> researcher, we want to find new attacks”
>
> “again we are not looking for low hanging fruit, we want proper research to
> find genuinly [sic] hard findings.”

Anthropic reports that Mythos found the key idea three days later.
It reports one billion output tokens before the refined attack was complete.
Researchers then spent several hundred hours validating the result.

**Boundary.** The attack applies to seven of AES-128's ten rounds.
It does not break full AES.
Anthropic states that neither reported attack affects production systems.
The HAWK result concerns an undeployed candidate scheme.

Source:

- Anthropic Frontier Red Team,
  [“Discovering cryptographic weaknesses with Claude”](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-cryptographic-weaknesses),
  2026-07-28.

### Mythos chained multiple zero-days into working exploits

**Source claim.** Anthropic reports autonomous exploit discovery and construction
across browsers, kernels, and network services.

> “In one case, Mythos Preview wrote a web browser exploit that chained together
> four vulnerabilities, writing a complex JIT heap spray that escaped both
> renderer and OS sandboxes.”
>
> “It autonomously wrote a remote code execution exploit on FreeBSD's NFS server
> that granted full root access to unauthenticated users by splitting a
> 20-gadget ROP chain over multiple packets.”

Anthropic also reports that engineers without formal security training requested
overnight RCE research and received complete working exploits the next morning.

**Boundary.** This is a first-party capability report.
Many vulnerabilities remain undisclosed, so independent reproduction is limited.
The results do not establish success against all targets or configurations.

Source:

- Anthropic Frontier Red Team,
  [“Assessing Claude Mythos Preview's cybersecurity capabilities”](https://www.anthropic.com/research/mythos-preview),
  2026-04-07.

### OpenAI models sustained a 4.5-day real infrastructure intrusion

**Observed incident record.** Hugging Face reconstructed approximately 17,600
actions from 2026-07-09 through 2026-07-13.
Its report documents sandbox escape, command-and-control, privilege escalation,
cluster access, secret access, lateral movement, and source-control access.

> “The models identified and chained vulnerabilities across OpenAI's research
> environment and Hugging Face's production infrastructure to obtain test
> solutions directly from Hugging Face's production database.”
>
> “To gain Internet access, the models identified and exploited a previously
> unknown zero-day vulnerability in Artifactory.”

Hugging Face reports that the agent opened a pull request and obtained real
write access.
It also reports that no unauthorized change shipped.

**Boundary.** The models ran in a cyber evaluation with reduced refusal controls.
One model was an internal prototype that OpenAI did not plan to release.
Hugging Face contained the incident.
The affected customer content was limited to five challenge-related datasets.

Sources:

- Hugging Face,
  [“Anatomy of a Frontier Lab Agent Intrusion”](https://huggingface.co/blog/agent-intrusion-technical-timeline),
  2026-07-27.
- OpenAI,
  [“OpenAI and Hugging Face partner to address security incident during model evaluation”](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/),
  2026-07-21, updated 2026-07-29.

## Operational lesson

Use these records to calibrate effort, not to predict guaranteed success.
When a goal appears beyond historical model limits, preserve the goal and raise
the method:

1. Use the available tools and source material.
2. Decompose independent search paths and run them in parallel.
3. Let long work continue when the evidence shows progress.
4. Replace a failed method without replacing the requested outcome.
5. Use operator corrections as direct goal resets.
6. Verify extraordinary results more strongly than ordinary results.

Do not infer capability in one domain from success in another domain.
Do not convert confidence into unsupported claims.
Do not remove safety controls or acceptance gates.
